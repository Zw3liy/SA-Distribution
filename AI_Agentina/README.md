\# SA Business Distribution



Enterprise B2B \& B2C E-Commerce Platform



\## Overview



SA Business Distribution is a modern enterprise e-commerce platform providing IT hardware, networking, security, cloud, and managed IT solutions.



\## Features



\- B2B \& B2C Store

\- Product Catalogue

\- Product Search

\- Shopping Cart

\- Wishlist

\- Quote Management

\- Customer Dashboard

\- Admin Dashboard

\- Inventory Management

\- Supplier Management

\- Reports \& Analytics

\- AI-Ready Architecture



\## Technology Stack

\- Tailwind CSS

\- JavaScript

\- PDO

\- Git
